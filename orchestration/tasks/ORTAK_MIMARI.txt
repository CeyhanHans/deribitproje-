- financial amounts/prices Decimal, JSON decimal-string. Float analitik metrik olabilir, NaN/Infinity JSON'a yazılmaz. Para birimi ve miktar birimi her alanda açık.
- Veri eksikliği sıfır fiyat değildir. complete_download, observed_no_trade, not_listed, incomplete_download, malformed ayrılır. Metadata uygunluğu likidite/gerçek dolum kanıtı değildir.

- HistoricalTradeProvider.fetch(plan)->DownloadResult; InstrumentProvider.load(snapshot)->Sequence[Instrument]; PriceProvider.iter_events(start_ms,end_ms,instruments)->Iterator[PriceObservation]; SettlementProvider.get(instrument,expiry_ms)->SettlementObservation|None; Store.put/get/query + resume protokolü.
- select_legs(strategy,asof_view)->SelectionDecision; evaluate_signals(config,history_asof,portfolio_view)->tuple[Signal,...]; simulate_fill(order,eligible_observations,execution_config,fee_schedule)->FillDecision; apply_fill(state,fill)->PortfolioState; settle(state,settlement)->PortfolioState; run(config,data_bundle)->RunResult; compute_metrics(result)->Metrics; write_report(result,path)->ReportManifest. - Contract: instrument_name, option_type, strike_usd, creation_ms, expiry_ms, base/quote/settlement_currency, contract_size, min_qty, qty_step, tick_schedule, source. Qty_step bilinmiyorsa min_qty=step varsayımı yapma; unknown flag/policy.

- PriceObservation: instrument_name, observed_at_ms, available_at_ms, price_basis, trade/bid/ask/mark opsiyonel Decimal alanları, size alanları, interval_start/end, underlying/index reference, quality/source_ref. Null alan türetilmiş gerçek veri diye doldurulmaz.
